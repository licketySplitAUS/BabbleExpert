Make yourself at home

A quick guide to Babble Expert. Read it whenever you like—your puzzle stays open.

  1. Choose your clues

    Choose Lickety’s original list or Self Serve’s generated clues from the Clues menu. Sign in to PlayBabble in the game pane. Expert remembers your choice.

  2. Enter words with Lickety Add

    Open Lickety Add, type a word (4+ letters) and press Enter. Text with spaces goes to chat unless No Chat is on. Ctrl+Enter (Mac: ⌘+Enter) forces chat.

    • Green: new word confirmed by Babble.
    • Amber: duplicate or not confirmed in Babble—read the message.
    • Red: word rejected.
    • Blue: chat submitted; check the chat pane.

  3. Bring your word lists together

    Send Words to Clues marks words found in Babble. Send Words to Babble submits checked clue words still missing there. Transfers wait for matching grids.

Move or resize Lickety Add to suit you. Need a key reminder? Open Keyboard shortcuts above.
