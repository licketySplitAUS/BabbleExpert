Make yourself at home

A quick guide to Babble Expert. Read it whenever you like—your puzzle stays open.

Watch the guided tour above or choose Options → Watch guided tour. Explore entering words, clues, resources, chat, arranging your workspace and sharing clues. Pause whenever you like; choosing a chapter starts playing it immediately. The tour uses recordings of the real app and leaves your puzzle untouched.

  1. Choose your clues

    Choose Lickety’s original list or Self Serve’s generated clues from the Clues menu. Sign in to PlayBabble in the game pane. Expert remembers your choice.

  2. Enter words with Lickety Add

    Open Lickety Add, type a word (4+ letters) and press Enter. Normal messages with spaces go to chat with Enter. Text that looks like a Babble word list stays in the box; use Ctrl+Enter (Mac: ⌘+Enter) only if you meant to send it as chat. Multiline entries and text over 500 characters are blocked and kept for editing. Expert syncs your words automatically; Bulk Add / Grab is hidden in the embedded clues pages.

    • Green: new word confirmed by Babble.
    • Amber: duplicate or not confirmed in Babble—read the message.
    • Red: word rejected.
    • Blue: chat submitted; check the chat pane.

  3. Bring your word lists together

    Words automatically sync in both directions when the grids match, including saved words and after switching clue sources. Use Send Words to Clues or Send Words to Babble for a manual sync. Typing and pasting highlight the Babble grid. You can also click adjacent Babble letters to build a word, then press Enter or Add.

Drag the dotted yellow dividers to resize the panes. Use Options → Zoom to enlarge or reduce all three pages, or choose Normal Size (100%) to reset. Your zoom is remembered. Move or resize Lickety Add to suit you. Need a key reminder? Open Keyboard shortcuts above.

New ways to play

  • Send lickety a message. Open Options → Send lickety a message, write up to 500 characters, and choose Send message. Include your name or contact details if you would like a reply. The separate Babble username checkbox is optional and starts unticked: it links the username you enter to this installation for future reports, only while usage sharing is enabled. A saved link can be removed on the same page. The window confirms delivery and keeps your text if you need to retry.
  • Keep both clue sources up to date. Switching between Lickety’s Clues and Self Serve brings across your accepted Babble words. Sync also catches up saved words and retries temporary connection failures. Self Serve keeps your All, On or Off filter selected.
  • Build words by clicking the Babble grid. Click adjacent tiles, then press Enter or Add in Babble’s entry box. Qu enters both letters. Click the last selected tile again to remove it; Qu removes both letters. This undo also works on the clues grids. A click that cannot continue the old path starts a fresh word. Submissions from Lickety Add, Clues and word lists also fill Babble’s entry box.
  • Browse 4–12 letter word lists. Open a list from Resources. Type starting letters to centre a match; typing takes priority over gentle mouse-hover scrolling. Click a word to submit it. A light gray background means a possible conflict with the reference Boggle dice described on Lickety Info; hover for its reason. The word remains clickable, and confirmed result colours take priority. The − / + buttons beside Hide change word length and centre the closest starting-letter match to the last word you clicked. Before your first click, they use the middle word instead. With a list open, click an unfinished clue entry on either clue page to find the first word of its length and starting letters between its solved neighbours. Press N or right-click outside a text field in Clues or the word list to advance to the next unfinished clue and position both panes. Press P to go to the previous unfinished clue. Completed clues are skipped, and navigation wraps at either end. With Clues set to Off, completing the selected clue automatically moves both panes to the next unfinished clue while a word list is open, switching word length when needed. Text fields keep normal typing and editing menus. Mouse-wheel and middle-button scrolling remain available.
  • Recover recent entries. Open Entries… to review the last 50 entries sent through Lickety Add or resource word lists during this app session. Restore an entry to edit it or retry a word that was not confirmed. Restoring a chat draft does not send it again.
  • Create clues for words you have found. Options → Create Clues - click on your found word is on by default. Click anywhere on a completed word’s row in Babble’s My Word List, or click a found word or its row in Lickety’s Clues or Self Serve. Found/unfound checkboxes keep their normal action. A popup looks up a definition and adds the word’s starting letters, length and points, such as re4,4 : for “rees”. Choose Try again for another definition when available, or edit the clue yourself. Send to Babble Chat sends the displayed clue only when you click it. Close the popup with Close, its window close button or Esc. This feature sends the clicked word to Datamuse for its definition and needs an internet connection.
  • Celebrate and explore. Finishing a puzzle starts fireworks across the panes. Click the fireworks page to replay or Hide to dismiss it. Try Fun Word Play and Chill Vibes from Options for more visual effects.

Pages and logins

Options → Browser tools has a menu for each open pane. Return takes you back to its starting page; Back and Forward follow its history. Reload current page keeps its address, and Reload current page ignoring cache fetches fresh files. Clues reloads preserve checked words when its grid is unchanged.

Clear cached files (keep logins) keeps login cookies and saved site data. Babble and Clues share this cache; Resources has a separate session. Saved dictionary lists are separate from the browser cache; use Reload current page ignoring cache to request fresh words. Reset this site’s login and data… asks for confirmation, signs you out and removes the current site’s saved data, which can include local clue progress.

Options → Resources on the left moves Resources to the far left and remembers your choice. It starts on the right. Switch Sides (Clues and Babble) swaps those two pages independently. Moving Resources keeps the current page, word position and colours. Its resize bar supports dragging and Left/Right keys on either side.

Options → Personal progress journal… opens your puzzle history. Recording is on by default and stays on this device, building up found counts, words by length, longest words, completion and active playing time as you play. Puzzles partly tracked are labelled. Pause recording to keep your history without adding to it; export CSV or clear the journal from its window. Closing the journal window does not change your recording preference.

Expert’s logins are separate from your normal browser. Expert does not save passwords, and websites control how long a login lasts. Babble links return to the same HTTPS address and Babble pane, including links back from Iron Sudoku. Page and session information shows the current address and session.

What to expect when Expert updates

Installed editions check at startup and every 30 minutes. Use Options → Check for updates whenever you like: the top bar shows checking, the result or an error. “Up to date” and manual-check errors clear after 15 seconds; no extra buttons remain when there is no update.

On Windows

New versions download in the background. Once ready, choose:

  • Update now / Install and restart: saves settings and closes Expert. An installer shows progress, then reopens Expert automatically. Wait for it to reopen instead of starting it from the shortcut.
  • Later: keeps Expert open. Closing it later installs silently without reopening. Allow the installation to finish before using the shortcut again.

Your desktop shortcut stays in place; its presence does not mean installation is finished. The first launch after a detected upgrade briefly confirms the new version in the top bar. Older installations without a saved version show the running version instead.

On Mac

Choose Download DMG, quit Expert, open the download and replace Expert in Applications, then reopen it. Mac updates are not installed automatically.

If a check or download fails, keep playing and try Check for updates again later. If Expert does not reopen after Update now, wait until the installer has finished before using the desktop shortcut.
