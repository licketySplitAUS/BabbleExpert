How this record works

Active time is recorded while the Babble puzzle is available and the main Expert window or Lickety Add has focus. It pauses when minimised, locked, asleep, or after five minutes without device input. Time outside Expert is excluded.

Found-word counts include words already present when tracking starts. Partly tracked puzzles are labelled; active time is the time this journal recorded, not your complete solving time. Longest words and counts come from Babble’s confirmed word list. Up to 2,000 puzzle records are kept.

Closing this window keeps recording enabled. Untick the recording option to pause it and keep your history. A CSV export gives you a copy to keep or move to another device.
