## What's new in Babble Expert 3.2.11

- **Send lickety a message:** separate optional boxes now let you add your name or Babble username and an email address for replies. Your message still allows up to 500 characters.
- **Keep your draft:** your message and contact details remain available if sending fails, so you can retry.

## Downloads and updates

- **Windows:** use Check for updates in Options, or run `BabbleExpert-Windows-Setup.exe`.
- **Mac:** open `BabbleExpert-macOS.dmg` and replace Babble Expert in Applications. The universal app supports Intel and Apple Silicon.

The installers are unsigned, as with previous releases. Follow the existing installation guidance if Windows or macOS asks for confirmation.
