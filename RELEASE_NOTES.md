## What's new in Babble Expert 3.3.0

- **Faster word lists:** Resources renders the words in view, keeping large lists responsive. Validated dictionary lists are saved on your device for quick reopening and offline use after the first download. Saved lists refresh in the background when needed.
- **Word colours that stay with you:** green means added to Babble, amber means already found or not yet confirmed, and red means not accepted. Colours survive changing word lengths and reopening Resources, and reset for a new puzzle. Words already found in Babble are recognised too.
- **Move through clues with N and P:** with a letter-word resource open, press **N** or right-click outside a text field for the next unfinished clue. Press **P** for the previous one. Clues and Resources position together, skip completed clues and wrap at either end. Normal typing and editing menus are preserved.
- **Resources on the left:** choose **Options → Resources on the left** to move Resources to the far left. Your preference is remembered. Swapping Clues and Babble, resizing, zoom, the current word and its colours continue to work.
- **Personal progress journal:** choose **Options → Personal progress journal** to enable an optional record of puzzles, found counts by length, longest words, observed completion and active playing time. Recording starts off. Records stay on your device, with CSV export and confirmed deletion. Partly tracked puzzles are labelled; this is recorded active time, not a claim about your complete solving time.
- **A short introduction after updates:** a bundled **What's new** window highlights useful new features. **OK** acknowledges that version once on your device. **Options → What’s new** reopens it, and full release notes remain in Help. Closing Help and What’s new returns focus to Expert.
- **Clear loading feedback and safer entry:** a centred loading glyph appears while Resources finds the right word and respects reduced-motion preferences. Lickety Add blocks multiline, oversized and likely pasted word lists from accidentally reaching chat; ordinary chat and explicit Ctrl+Enter (Mac: ⌘+Enter) remain available.

Journal records, puzzle words and journal timing are never sent to Discord. If anonymous usage sharing is enabled, moving Resources left and enabling journal recording send only the corresponding option-use notice with the existing anonymous usage fields.

## Downloads and updates

The installed version is **V3.3.0.0**. Windows users can update from Options or the official Setup download. Mac users replace Babble Expert in Applications using the new universal DMG, supporting Intel and Apple Silicon. Existing settings and logins are preserved; the new journal starts disabled until you choose to enable it.

Both installers remain unsigned by the maintainer's choice. Follow the existing installation guide for system prompts. Older Expert releases continue to use the existing resource and Clues websites.
