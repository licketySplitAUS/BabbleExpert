## What's new in Babble Expert 4.0.0

- **Removed Entries:** Removed the Entries feature, as it wasn’t very useful. Lickety Add still keeps your last entry available with Up Arrow.
- **Words Left at a glance:** Moved the Words Left count to the right, with a rainbow level and changing colours to show completion.
- **42 surprise celebrations:** Enjoy new celebrations, with future effects delivered automatically. Play again repeats the celebration you just saw.
- **Clearer personal progress:** Past unfinished puzzles are now marked Incomplete in red.
- **Copy today’s progress:** Copy today’s puzzle letters, found words and progress in one summary.
- **Quieter update recovery:** Temporary update failures retry quietly. Routine offline failures stay quiet too.

## Downloads and updates

The installed version is **V4.0.0.0**. Windows users can update from Options or the official Setup download. Mac users replace Babble Expert in Applications using the universal DMG, supporting Intel and Apple Silicon. Existing settings, logins and journal history are preserved.

Both installers remain unsigned by the maintainer’s choice. Follow the existing installation guide for system prompts.
