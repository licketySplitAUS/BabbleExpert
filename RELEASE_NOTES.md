## What's new in Babble Expert 3.2.4

- **A guided tour using the real application:** a ten-chapter walkthrough covers entering words, clues, resources, chat, layouts, Chill mode, Fun Word Play and Create Clues. Watch at your own pace with captions, progress, chapter navigation, playback speed and reduced motion. Expert offers the tour after signing in; replay it from Help or Options. Selecting a chapter always starts playback.
- **Word entry that stays together:** enter words in Babble, Lickety Add, either clues page or by clicking a resource word. Accepted words sync between Babble and clues, and switching clues pages brings your found words across. Self Serve keeps your selected All / On / Off filter.
- **Clickable grids and letter highlights:** click letters in the Babble or clues grid to build your word. Typing in word-entry boxes highlights the matching letters in Babble.
- **Easier resource browsing:** typing centres matching words and takes priority over mouse movement. Gentler hover scrolling makes navigation steadier. The + and - controls change word length using your last clicked word as the starting point. Clicking an unfinished clue finds candidates with its length and starting letters, between nearby solved clues.
- **Create Clues:** enable ?Create Clues - click on your found word? in Options, then click a found word in Babble or either clues page. Read and edit a definition-based clue, try another, close the popup or choose Send to Babble Chat. This feature is off by default.
- **Updated Help:** expanded instructions, video links and explanations of the new entry, resource and clue-sharing features.
- **Refined optional usage notifications:** letter resources report only once per session. Opted-in notifications also cover clue-source switches, Self Serve Grab Grid and user-triggered fireworks.
- **Entry and login safeguards:** login forms stay separate from word-entry features. This release also corrects keyboard forwarding to interactive resources after the puzzle is ready.

## Downloads and updates

- **Windows:** use Check for updates in Options, or run `BabbleExpert-Windows-Setup.exe`.
- **Mac:** open `BabbleExpert-macOS.dmg` and replace Babble Expert in Applications. The universal app supports Intel and Apple Silicon.

The installers are unsigned, as with previous releases. Follow the existing installation guidance in Help if Windows or macOS asks for confirmation.
