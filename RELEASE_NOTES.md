## Babble Expert 3.2.3

- Exclude login and account fields from word-entry highlighting, so Email and Password fields are handled solely by the Babble website.
- Stop forwarding Babble keystrokes to interactive resources while the puzzle is not ready.
- Add regression checks for login field detection and unchanged form submissions and cookies.

This release addresses a possible source of login interference. The reported Mac credential rejection has not yet been reproduced; affected users are invited to retest. Babble requires your account email address when signing in.

## Installing

- Windows: use the updater or run BabbleExpert-Windows-Setup.exe.
- Mac: open BabbleExpert-macOS.dmg and replace Babble Expert in Applications. The universal app supports Intel and Apple Silicon.

Installers remain unsigned, as with previous releases.
